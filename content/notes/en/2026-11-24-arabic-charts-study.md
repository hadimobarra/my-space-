---
title: "No chart tool mirrored by default"
date: "2026-11-24"
---

A peer-reviewed study tested seven popular chart tools with Arabic data and interviewed eleven practitioners. None of the tools produced a mirrored chart by default, and Eastern numerals were often read as text. [Designing Within the Lines](https://kclpure.kcl.ac.uk/ws/files/383439862/2026_VIS_RTL_Interviews_tools_revised_-2.pdf) is worth reading if you build dashboards.
